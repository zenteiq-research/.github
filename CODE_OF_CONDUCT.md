# Code of Conduct

ZenteiQ Research projects bring together researchers, engineers, students and practitioners from many institutions and countries. This code of conduct sets out how we expect everyone to behave so that people can do their best scientific work together.

It applies to all repositories in the [zenteiq-research](https://github.com/zenteiq-research) organisation and to every project space connected to them: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project chat channels, and events where someone represents a ZenteiQ Research project.

## Our commitment

We want participation in our projects to be open to everyone and free from harassment, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, language, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and considerate, especially when you disagree. Critique ideas, methods and code, never people.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors work in a second or third language, and tone is easy to misread in text.
- Give credit. Acknowledge the work, data and ideas of others, and cite them properly.
- Be honest about results. Report what the evidence shows, including negative results, limitations and uncertainty.
- Help newcomers. Answer questions patiently and point people to documentation rather than dismissing them.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and take responsibility for mistakes.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Insulting, demeaning or discriminatory language or imagery, including sexualised language or attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as contact details or location, without their explicit permission.
- Misrepresenting results, fabricating data, or plagiarising code, text or figures.
- Sharing confidential, proprietary or export-controlled material in a public project space.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional scientific setting.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **info@zenteiq.ai** with the subject line `[Conduct] <project name>`. Please include what happened, where (with links if possible), and who was involved.

Reports are handled by the conduct team, made up of organisation owners and designated maintainers. Anyone named in a report, or with a conflict of interest, is excluded from handling it. We will keep the reporter's identity confidential as far as the situation allows, and we will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

We aim to acknowledge reports within five working days.

## How we respond

The conduct team decides on a response after reviewing the report and, where appropriate, speaking with the people involved. Responses are proportionate and may include:

1. **A private note.** A clarification of expectations, with a request to change behaviour or edit a comment.
2. **A formal warning.** A written warning stating the consequences of further incidents.
3. **A temporary ban.** Removal from all project spaces for a set period.
4. **A permanent ban.** Removal from all project spaces with no return.

Maintainers may also edit, hide or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and they will explain why when it is safe to do so.
